FAQ

General

Q: What is Pesterfest?

A: Pesterfest is an online convention for fans of Homestuck and other MS Paint Adventures projects, created entirely by volunteers. Over the course of three days in 2026, we will provide a digital space to celebrate our shared love of these stories and fan projects.

Q: When will the convention take place?

A: Pesterfest 2026 will take place from July 24–26, 2026.

Q: Where will the convention take place?

A: The convention will be held entirely online. The Artist’s Alley will take place exclusively on our Discord server, while the Project Showcase and panels will be hosted across Discord, this website, and YouTube.

Q: What if I can’t attend the convention live, but still want to experience the events?

A: We will maintain an archive of artist lineups, panels, project showcases, and other Pesterfest events on this website. Our goal is to make Pesterfest accessible to everyone, regardless of timezone.

Q: How much does it cost to attend?

A: Pesterfest is a non-profit event run entirely by volunteers, so attendance and all events are completely free.

Events

Q: What events will be held at Pesterfest?

A: Pesterfest will feature several events, including:

  • Artist’s Alley — A collection of artists selling Homestuck-related merchandise and commissions.
  • Project Showcase — Trailers, teasers, and presentations for Homestuck-related fan projects, including fan adventures, games, and more.
  • Panels — Discussions hosted by fans covering various aspects of Homestuck.
  • Community Games — Online Homestuck-related games for staff and attendees to enjoy between scheduled events.

Q: What is the minimum age requirement to attend panels?

A: That depends on the panel. Each panel will be clearly labeled with its appropriate age rating.

Q: Will merchandise featuring Homestuck characters be sold in the Artist’s Alley?

A: Yes. We have received permission for Artist’s Alley contributors to advertise and sell merchandise based on Homestuck characters.

Volunteering

Q: How can I join the team?

A: Please visit our Contribute page for more information about joining the Pesterfest team.

Q: Will you be accepting more team members in the future?

A: Yes. Keep an eye on our social media for announcements about open positions. Team membership changes over time, so even if a role is not currently available, it may open up in the future.
